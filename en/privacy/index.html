Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Mermaid Live Editor (mermaid.baimuxym.cn) handles information.

What We Collect

The editor runs in your browser. Mermaid source you type is used only for local rendering and export; it is not sent to our servers for storage. We do not require registration or login.

Analytics and Advertising

We may add Google Analytics or Google AdSense in the future to understand traffic or show ads. If enabled, those services may use cookies and collect anonymous usage data. This page will be updated with specifics.

Third-Party Links

The header links to HelloCoder Blog (https://learnjava.baimuxym.cn/). Third-party sites have their own privacy policies.

Policy Updates

We may revise this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Privacy questions: contact us via the blog at https://learnjava.baimuxym.cn/
